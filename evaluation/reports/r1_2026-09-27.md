## Interpretation (Curation & Evaluation Lead #1)
1. **Model choice: OpenCLIP xlm-roberta B/32 beats SigLIP 2 base on this library,** mainly in Hebrew: P@10 0.57 vs 0.33, nDCG@20 0.56 vs 0.32. SigLIP 2 in Hebrew often returns nothing relevant (P@10 = 0 on selfie/hugging/couple/dog). In English the two are close (P@10 0.71 each). Note: OpenCLIP is POC_ONLY (LAION provenance, MODEL_REGISTRY), so a commercially clean multilingual model is still needed.
2. **Hebrew is weaker than English even for the multilingual model** (P@10 0.57 vs 0.71). Single-word Hebrew fails badly: "ים" gives P@10 0.10 in both models vs 0.90 in English. Cheap next test: translate the query to English locally before searching, and compare on the same grades.
3. **Redundancy is high** (dup@20 up to 0.6–0.8 on city at night / couple at a landmark): the top results are near-copies. Ranking needs a diversity step (MMR / one per duplicate group), which is also a curation requirement.
4. **Must-find recall@20 looks low (~0.3) partly by construction:** 34 must-find sunsets can't fit in a top 20 (max 0.59). Read R@50 (~0.5) alongside it; the next round reports recall@max(20, n).
5. **Owner feedback on wording (2026-09-27):** several queries were narrower than what the owner actually wants (dog vs. dog on the couch, sunset vs. sunset at the beach, airport AND airplane, food vs. food on a table). He graded by intent, so these scores partly penalize the phrasing. For the next round, reword the TUNING queries only. The held-out queries (selfie_two, couple_landmark, pasta_pizza) stay frozen, or the held-out check loses its meaning.
6. **Why the dog photos "didn't appear" on the grading page:** the 4 must-find dog photos were removed from grading (they count as ★ automatically). OpenCLIP actually ranks all 4 in its top 20 (R@20 = 1.00); the other ~50 candidates were all graded 0.
7. **Limits:** one library, 12 queries (3 held out, easy ones: P@10 = 1.0), MUST_FIND chosen from a pooled candidate set (depth 30 + 20 random per query, the owner's request), so recall is relative to that pool. Grade 1 "partial" was dropped for speed.
